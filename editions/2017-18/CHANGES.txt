Forza-WCCF 2017-18 - release notes
==================================

0.4.1 - 2026-10-26 (alpha)
--------------------------
Closing the projector window keeps the game going; panel hotkeys show on Alt.

  - Closing the projector window no longer stops the game. The seat keeps playing.
  - Panel: the buttons around the game picture show their hotkeys only while Alt is held.
  - After UPDATE the Launcher restarts as the new version, so setup, game text and card pictures are made by the new code.
    This takes effect from the update after 0.4.1: updating to 0.4.1 is done by the 0.4.0 Launcher, so close and reopen
    the Launcher once after updating.
  - Updated libraries: iroh 1.4.1, tokio 1.54.1 and other crates.

0.4.0 - 2026-10-25 (alpha)
--------------------------
Album card art, key latch and drag-off in the panel, a choice before a CPU match, installer and keys fixes.

  - Card pictures come from the 2017-18 game's own album art for 2,875 cards (12-13 to 17-18), sharper than the HQ pack.
    They are made from your own files, and made again at PLAY after an update.
  - Plainer text in English, Italian and Japanese, and no question after UPDATE.
  - Panel: right-click KEY PLAYER or KEEPER to hold the key. Drag a card off the board to remove it, with UNDO.
  - Before a CPU match you choose KEEP WAITING or PLAY CPU NOW.
  - Panel: keycaps on the deck buttons. KEYS (operator keys) are under Advanced; TEST is unbound by default.
  - Offline lobby: numbers blanked, RETRY, and a reason when JOIN is grey.
  - The missing-files message names the file.
  - A bench card held on the table moves when you rub it from its outer edge.
  - Installer: the package is marked as needed, the newest is the default, START LAUNCHER at the end, a SmartScreen hint.

0.3.3 - 2026-10-24 (alpha)
--------------------------
Club progress kept between plays, the shop, Best Eleven, problem reports.

  - Your club keeps its league, division and season between plays, and your players keep their levels. Before, a
    promoted club was back in league 2 at the next card-in.
  - The shop opens. Items you buy are kept and applied to your club.
  - Best Eleven is kept between plays.
  - Report a problem, in Launcher: say what happened, and the logs go to the project with your user and PC names
    removed. You get a report number. Without a connection the report is kept.
  - PLAY / STOP THE GAME and the status line are at the bottom of the Launcher window. Network errors are in plain
    words.
  - Launcher shows when a new version is out, on its main page and on UPDATE, until you open UPDATE.
  - A game window that closes normally no longer shows as crashed.
  - Panel: the formation board looks like the cabinet's mat (FW, MF, DF, the keeper on the goal line).
  - Panel: quitting during a match asks first. KEEP PLAYING is the default.
  - Panel: when the game loses its server, a screen offers RESTART THE GAME and SEND LOGS.
  - Panel: a bench card held on the table moves when you rub it.
  - Catalogue cards and the large card on hover are sized for your screen.
  - English and Italian names for the CPU and guest teams, the clubs and the nationalities.

0.3.2 - 2026-10-23 (alpha)
--------------------------
In-app updates, a crash fix and catalogue fixes from player reports.

  - UPDATE in the Launcher finds new Forza-WCCF versions and installs them in place, checked against their published
    SHA-256. The Launcher looks once a day and says when a new version is out.
  - No more stop on the secretary screen when making a new club in Italian. A game text that is longer than its box
    is now cut to fit instead of stopping the game.
  - After copying a new version over an old one, the Launcher builds the game text again at the next start, so the
    game never keeps an older version's text.
  - The catalogue search finds MVP cards ("ronaldinho mvp"), short seasons ("0506", "12/13") and rarities ("regular",
    "normale", "レギュラー").
  - A team holds one card per player: adding a second card of a player from the catalogue is refused with a message,
    and a card dropped on the board takes the place of that player's other card.
  - The high-quality card picture pack works: in Launcher, CARD PICTURES, "Choose pack..." takes the pack ZIP
    (forza-wccf-card-pictures-hq-*.zip). Before, Launcher refused it, and pictures unzipped by hand were removed at
    the next setup.
  - The programs no longer contain folder paths of the PC that built them.

0.3.0 - 2026-10-23 (alpha)
--------------------------
Movies, saved player growth and fixes from player reports.

  - Movies play on PCs with many CPU threads. Before, no movie ever played there, and a cup match stayed on a black
    screen until START was pressed. The cup now starts by itself.
  - The keyboard keeps working after the pre-match screen closes.
  - Your players keep their experience, level, confidence and form between plays. Before, every player went back to
    level 0 at the next card-in. Players reset by an earlier version start again from their next save.
  - No more "Error 3000 WCCF CONTROL NOT FOUND" after about 45 minutes of play: the server program crashed on download
    data the stand-in sent with the wrong size.
  - The World Players List registers every QR card 3 times (943 of the 1,000 places), the most the game allows, so
    special coaching gets the full bonus.
  - Launcher.exe and its shortcuts have the Forza-WCCF logo, the lobby lists the server's shops (view only), and the
    SAVE LOGS file name includes the time.

0.2.2 - 2026-10-22 (alpha)
--------------------------
Crash fixes for testers.

  - Italian: the game no longer ends (error 0xC0000417) right after the shirt design. The secretary's birthday text
    was too long for the game; it now reads month/day. A friend-mission notice that was also too long is shorter.
  - When the game crashes or closes by itself, Launcher.exe says so, asks you to save the logs at once and tells you
    where to report it.
  - At home, a seat that crashes now stops the projector too.
  - Launcher.exe draws its window with OpenGL first (Vulkan showed a black window or none on some graphics cards), and
    its window's lines go to data\logs\launcher.txt, which SAVE LOGS includes.
  - The panel's position map shows each area of the pitch as a tile, coloured by how well the player fits it, like the
    fan database. FITS and the out-of-position mark follow the tiles.
  - forza_panel.log lists the overlay and capture programs hooked into the game (Discord, Steam, OBS, RivaTuner,
    NVIDIA, Fraps), which helps with black-screen reports.

0.2.0 - 2026-10-21 (alpha)
--------------------------
Online play, the lobby, no Python.

  - No Python: setup, the card catalogue, the card pictures and the game's text run inside Launcher.exe. Forza-WCCF has no
    python\ or scripts\ folder.
  - Online play: choose Online in Launcher.exe. The game uses the game server you name (the "Club server" row, else the
    lobby's server) instead of the stand-in on this PC; "This PC" goes back to the stand-in. Remote and server modes
    stay 2010-11 only.
  - The lobby lists VS ROOMS (SHOPS stays greyed for 2017-18). HOST VS makes a room with a 4-letter code, QUICK MATCH
    takes the oldest open room or opens one, READY on both sides starts a 5-second countdown, and a lone READY for about
    90 seconds starts the game with a CPU team. Keyboard (Up/Down, Left/Right or Tab, Enter, H, F5, Esc) and gamepad
    (D-pad or left stick, A, B, X, Y, LB/RB). Texts in English, Italian and Japanese.
  - A VS room connects its two PCs over the direct VS link (relay.wccf.it) when the firewall allows it, else over the
    relay.
  - Online, each card gets its own Aime ID, derived from the card's code: two homes never share one club. On "This PC"
    existing clubs keep their IDs.
  - The Club server and Direct VS link rows moved from SETUP into ONLINE's "Direct address" fold.
  - Pause: P or the PAUSE button holds a match for up to 20 seconds. Not online, not with another seat linked, not while
    a projector runs. Key: KEYS.
  - The installer's package step takes this edition's ZIP (it must match the game's edition).
  - No overlay\inject.exe (antivirus programs flagged it) and no netguard.dll: the game loads the panel through
    wccfnet.dll. Run Setup again after the update.

Not in this release: career mode, LAN play, shops with several seats, a render scale. Online needs a server that allows
your address (the project's server keeps an allow list). Live VS between two PCs has not been played end to end yet.

0.1.0-alpha.1
-------------
First alpha: the 2017-18 game plays offline on one PC, one seat, from your own game files.

  - Game server stand-in: a server on this PC answers the game's club and save requests. Clubs and saves are kept in
    data\save\gamesvr and survive restarts (new card, tutorial, club set-up, matches, restart: the same club loads, no
    error 3107).
  - Rare 2D-code cards work in the World Players List and in team registration.
  - Skip the tutorial: a new card gets a ready club and goes to the main screen.
  - New Rust panel in the cabinet's look: CENTRAL / COUNTER / L-SIDE / R-SIDE triangles, PRESS, DATA, SHOOT, KEEPER,
    KEY PLAYER and START buttons, formation board, keys.
  - Card pictures, made at setup from your game files (or the optional high-quality pack).
  - Game text in English and Italian, as well as the original Japanese.
  - The network guard: the game's network calls are stubbed, and Launcher.exe refuses to start the game without it.

Known issues: the control program may crash after about 90 minutes idle; registering a team may wait about 95 seconds;
the programs are unsigned. Not in this alpha: career mode, online or LAN play, installer support for 2017-18, pause,
render scale. See README.txt.

Versions after this one go above this line.
